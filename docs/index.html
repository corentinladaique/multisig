Multisig

Shared vaults on Solana.
Everyone signs, nobody trusts alone.

  • 2-of-3 by default. A transfer needs more than one key.
  • Keys never leave your wallet. Seed Vault, Solflare or a Ledger — every signature happens there, never in this app.
  • Read-only by design. The app holds no secret and no backend.
